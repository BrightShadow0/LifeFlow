The thinking behind LifeFlow

Everything has a place.
Everything has a purpose.

Why it exists. How it works. What it makes possible. One continuous journey, from scattered thoughts to a clearer way forward.

Life is bigger
than a task list.

Planning tools can make people manage lists instead of managing their lives. LifeFlow brings tasks, commitments, time and progress together for people balancing work or study, responsibilities and personal priorities.

One place for all the moving parts.
WHAT IT HOLDSYour time · Attention · Commitments · Progress

See what matters.
Know what comes next.

In your first 30 seconds, you should understand what needs attention today, what is already handled and where your time is going. Tasks, calendar and board bring the day into view without demanding that you constantly keep up.

Clear. Calm. Focused. Human. In control.
THE FEELINGLess friction. More room to move.

Ideas are better
when they connect.

Notes Manager gives you a Markdown-powered space for headings, formatting, lists, checklists, links, tables and code. Connect notes with [[Title]] links, then explore the relationships in Your Branches. Your notes, tasks and settings stay in your browser, close to you.

Local by design. Your thinking, made visible.
WHY IT MATTERSKeep ideas close. Follow the connections.

Make space
for momentum.

Flow means moving through life with less friction: seeing what is next, giving attention to the right things and continuously making progress. Six months from now, success is fewer forgotten commitments, clearer days and meaningful progress, not a perfectly maintained planner.

LifeFlow should help you decide, not just record.
ONE PRINCIPLEA task should help you take the next meaningful step.
