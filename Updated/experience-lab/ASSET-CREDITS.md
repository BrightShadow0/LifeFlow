# 3D asset credits

The current Experience Lab builds its road, touring car, forest, roadside details, terrain and atmospheric sky directly with Three.js geometry and physically based materials.

No third-party vehicle, vegetation or road models are loaded by the scene. Three.js and its RoomEnvironment helper are loaded from jsDelivr. The synthetic environment map is used for material reflections.

The scene geometry, surface textures, layout, lighting setup, camera path and scroll choreography are custom LifeFlow preview code.
