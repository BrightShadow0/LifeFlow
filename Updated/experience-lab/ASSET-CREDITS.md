# 3D asset credits

The 3D vehicle, trees, shrubs and modular road pieces in this preview are from Kenney's CC0 asset packs.

- **Car Kit** — https://kenney.nl/assets/car-kit
- **Nature Kit** — https://kenney.nl/assets/nature-kit
- **City Kit (Roads)** — https://kenney.nl/assets/city-kit-roads

These packs are released under CC0 1.0. Credit is not required; this note is included as a courtesy. The road spline, terrain, lighting, roadside layout, camera path and scroll choreography are custom LifeFlow preview code.

The models are loaded from the public Hidencod/tge-assets mirror of Kenney CC0 assets via jsDelivr.
