01 — WHY IT EXISTS

Life is biggerthan a task list.

Planning tools often make people manage lists instead of managing their lives. LifeFlow brings tasks, commitments, time and progress into one place.

THE PURPOSETasks · Commitments · Time · Progress

One space forevery side of life.

For people balancing multiple parts of life: work or study, responsibilities, personal priorities and the things they want to make progress on.

BUILT FORWork · Study · Responsibilities · Personal goals

More than tasks.Your whole day.

Tasks are the starting point, but the centre is broader: your time, attention, commitments and progress.

AT ITS CORETime · Attention · Commitments · Progress

Know what needsyour attention.

Quickly understand what needs attention today, what is already handled, and where your time is going.

AT A GLANCEToday's focus · What's handled · Your time

Clear. Calm.Focused. Human.

LifeFlow should feel clear, calm, focused, human and in control. It should never feel like another system demanding that you keep up with it.

THE FEELINGClarity · Calm · Focus · Control

Give your energyto what matters.

Flow means moving through life with less friction: seeing what is next, giving attention to the right things, and continuously making progress.

THE IDEASee what's next · Focus · Make progress

Progress thatyou can feel.

Success is not having a perfectly maintained planner. It is having fewer forgotten commitments, clearer days, better use of time, and a stronger sense of progress in the parts of life that matter.

SIX MONTHS LATERFewer forgotten things · Clearer days · Better progress

Your life staysclose to you.

LifeFlow runs locally in your browser, so your tasks, notes, plans and settings are stored on your device instead of being synced to a central LifeFlow server. Your life stays close to you.

LOCAL BY DESIGNYour browser · Your device · Your data

Ideas connect.Thoughts take shape.

Notes Manager gives you a flexible writing space with Markdown support. Format ideas with headings, bold and italic text, lists, checklists, links, tables and code blocks. Switch between Write and Preview to see your formatted notes as you work. Use double-square-bracket links like [[Project Planning]] to connect notes by title, with autocomplete to find existing notes. Open linked notes directly and explore the connections in Your Branches.

WRITE & CONNECTMarkdown · Linked Notes · Your Branches
