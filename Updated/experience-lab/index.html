Clear. Calm.
In control.

06 / WHY FLOW

Give your energy
to what matters.

Flow means moving through life with less friction: seeing what is next, giving attention to the right things, and continuously making progress.

KEEP MOVINGSee · Focus · Progress
07 / THE SIX-MONTH TEST

Progress you
can feel.

Success is fewer forgotten commitments, clearer days, better use of time and a stronger sense of progress, not a perfectly maintained planner.

THE LONG VIEWProgress over perfection

Your life stays
close to you.

LifeFlow runs locally in your browser. Your tasks, notes, plans and settings are stored on your device instead of being synced to a central LifeFlow server.

YOUR SPACEYour browser · Your device · Your data
