Life is bigger

Planning tools can make people manage lists instead of managing their lives. LifeFlow brings tasks, commitments, time and progress into one place.

For every side
of your life.

For people balancing multiple parts of life: work or study, responsibilities, personal priorities and the things they want to make progress on.

More than tasks.
Your whole day.

Tasks are the starting point, but the centre is broader: your time, attention, commitments and progress.

05 / HOW IT SHOULD FEEL

Clear. Calm.
In control.

Focused, human and calm. LifeFlow should never feel like another system demanding that you keep up with it.

THE FEELINGClarity · Calm · Focus · Control
06 / WHY FLOW

Give your energy
to what matters.

Flow means moving through life with less friction: seeing what is next, giving attention to the right things, and continuously making progress.

KEEP MOVINGSee · Focus · Make progress
07 / THE SIX-MONTH TEST

Progress you
can feel.

Success is fewer forgotten commitments, clearer days, better use of time and a stronger sense of progress, not a perfectly maintained planner.

THE LONG VIEWProgress over perfection
08 / LOCAL BY DESIGN

Your life stays
close to you.

LifeFlow runs locally in your browser. Your tasks, notes, plans and settings are stored on your device instead of being synced to a central LifeFlow server.

YOUR SPACEYour browser · Your device · Your data
